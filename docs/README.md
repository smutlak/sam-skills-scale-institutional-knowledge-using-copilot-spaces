# OctoAcme Project Management Processes

Welcome to the OctoAcme project management documentation. These guides describe a practical, collaborative approach for moving work from an initial idea to a released product and learning from the results.

## Project lifecycle

OctoAcme's process covers the following lifecycle:

1. **Initiation** — establish the purpose, goals, scope, stakeholders, and initial decision gate.
2. **Planning** — turn goals into an actionable backlog, plan iterations, and identify risks and dependencies.
3. **Execution and tracking** — deliver work in a regular team rhythm, maintain quality, and report progress.
4. **Risk management and communication** — keep a current risk register, communicate with stakeholders, and escalate blockers early.
5. **Release and deployment** — verify release readiness, deploy safely, document changes, and prepare for rollback or incidents.
6. **Retrospectives and continuous improvement** — reflect on outcomes, track improvement actions, and strengthen the process over time.
